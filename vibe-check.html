Inside each LIVE session

Arrive. Learn. Practice. Carry it forward.

Each LIVE Vibe Check gives students a welcoming place to check in and explore one useful idea connected to schoolwork, relationships, technology habits, rest, or everyday pressure. The session then turns that idea into a simple practice students can use in daily life.

🫧

Arrive and Check In

Students notice their current VIBE honestly and without judgment. Every experience is welcome, and sharing is optional.

🌿

Learn to Notice

Students learn to notice thoughts, feelings, body sensations, and shifts in attention without immediately reacting.

☀️

Practice and Carry It Forward

A guided meditation and optional reflection help students practice settling, noticing, and returning—an approach they can use whenever they need it.

Why regular practice matters

What students can strengthen over time.

Regular meditation gives students a repeatable way to pause, notice what is happening, and return with greater clarity.

Focused Attention

Notice when attention drifts and bring it back to schoolwork, a conversation, or the present moment.

Emotional Regulation

Recognize strong feelings and create space before choosing how to respond.

Responding to Stress

Use a familiar calming practice before a demanding task, performance, difficult moment, or sleep.

Greater Self-Awareness

Notice thoughts, feelings, body signals, and patterns with greater clarity and less judgment.

Empathy & Connection

Listen more attentively and relate to others with greater understanding and care.

Secular, inclusive, and open to all.

Vibe Check is offered as a practical mindfulness experience. No particular belief, background, or prior meditation experience is required.
