There is nothing to get right.

No meditation experience is required. Students may join from wherever they are, practice at their own pace, and return to a simple skill they can use at school, at home, and in hard moments.

Inside each session

A welcoming space to arrive, learn, and practice.

Each live Vibe Check gives school-aged students a calm place to arrive as they are, learn one useful idea, practice meditation, and reflect without pressure.

🫧

Check In

Students notice their current VIBE with honesty and without judgment. Every number is welcome.

🌿

Learn One Useful Idea

A brief, age-appropriate idea connects meditation to attention, thoughts, feelings, school, relationships, or daily life.

☀️

Meditate and Reflect

A guided meditation creates room to notice breath, body, thoughts, and attention, followed by optional reflection.

A practice for real life

Meditation students can use when life gets demanding.

Vibe Check gives students a short practice they can return to before schoolwork, a performance, sleep, a difficult conversation, or a stressful moment.

Settle

Begin with the natural breath and allow the body to become still enough to pay attention.

Notice

Recognize thoughts, feelings, and body sensations without needing to fix or follow each one.

Return

Gently bring attention back to the present, again and again. That return is the practice.
